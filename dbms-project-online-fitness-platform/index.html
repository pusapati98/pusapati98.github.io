Online Fitness Platform

Database Design · EER Model · Memberships · Sessions · Payments

Overview

A database-backed platform for fitness services that supports personalized workout plans, nutrition guidance, trainer interactions, payments, BMI tracking, referrals, and feedback. Core entities include Login, Customer, Trainer, Membership, WorkoutPlan, ExercisePlan, Exercises, Session, NutritionPlan, Payment, BMI, and Feedback.

Key Features

  • Personalized workout & nutrition plans
  • Membership tiers (online-only to premium with offline training)
  • Trainer sessions, feedback & ratings
  • Secure payments with referral discounts
  • BMI calculation & user progress tracking
