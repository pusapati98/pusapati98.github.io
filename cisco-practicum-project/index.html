Cisco Practicum Project

Santa Clara University Practicum Collaboration

Project Overview

This practicum was conducted in collaboration with Cisco Systems. Our team analyzed ~290,000 Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) cases from July 2022 – July 2023 to uncover opportunities for efficiency improvements, automation, and better customer support.

Key Objectives

  • Align engineer problem statements with customer resolutions using text similarity.
  • Identify automation opportunities in Cisco’s RMA process.
  • Analyze seasonality and cost patterns in RMA cases.
  • Optimize Small-Medium Business (SMB) channel effectiveness.
  • Recommend customer education resources to reduce repeat RMAs.

Key Insights & Recommendations

  • Automate Phone product family RMAs with high repair alignment scores.
  • Promote web-based RMA submissions for SMBs instead of manual ones.
  • Automate failed power supply approvals; keep manual check for “Call Home Device”.
  • Provide FAQs, tutorials, and guides to reduce repetitive case volumes.
  • Standardize resolution descriptions to improve text similarity automation.
